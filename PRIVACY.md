# PortScope Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 August 2026**

**Faini Made Inc.** ("Faini Made", "we", "us") builds PortScope. This policy
explains what the PortScope application does and does not do with information on
your Mac.

> **The short version.** PortScope has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry,
> no advertising, and no tracking. Everything it reads about your hardware stays
> on your Mac. It makes exactly two kinds of outbound network request: license
> activation/validation, and an update check — both only in the circumstances
> described below.

---

## 1. What PortScope reads on your Mac

To do its job, PortScope reads information the operating system exposes about
your computer and the things you plug into it, including:

- connected ports, cables, docks, hubs, displays, and USB devices, and their
  identity, capability, and link information;
- power delivery information, including adapter, cable, and negotiated values;
- storage devices, mounted volume names, free space, drive temperature, and
  drive health data;
- network interface names and byte counters for the purpose of showing live
  throughput; and
- measurements PortScope itself performs when you run a test.

**This information is processed locally, in memory, for display on your screen.
It is not transmitted to Faini Made.** We do not operate a server that receives
it, and we never see it.

## 2. What PortScope stores, and where

PortScope stores the following **on your Mac only**, in your user Library
(Application Support and Preferences):

- your settings and preferences;
- your saved Speed Test history, including measurements and drive temperatures;
- the Cable Health record, if you leave that feature enabled;
- your custom display names; and
- your license key and activation state, if you have a license.

You can view the size of these files, reveal them in the Finder, and erase the
Cable Health record from **Settings → General → Data**. Deleting the application
and these files removes all of it. We hold no copy.

## 3. When PortScope connects to the internet

PortScope makes no background network connections for analytics or tracking. It
connects only in these cases:

### 3.1 License activation, validation, and deactivation

If you purchase a license, PortScope contacts our licensing provider,
**Lemon Squeezy**, to activate, periodically validate, or deactivate your
license. These requests contain:

- your **license key**;
- your Mac's **computer name**, as set in macOS (for example "Brian's MacBook
  Pro"), which is sent as the activation's name so that **you** can tell your
  activated Macs apart and choose which one to deactivate; and
- an activation identifier returned by the provider.

Note that a computer name often contains a person's name. If you would prefer it
not be sent, change your Mac's name in **System Settings → General → About**
before activating.

These requests contain **no** information about your cables, drives, displays,
devices, test results, or files.

Lemon Squeezy also acts as merchant of record for purchases and processes your
payment and billing details directly; Faini Made does not receive or store your
payment card information. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy at
https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

### 3.2 Checking for updates

When you click **Check for Updates**, PortScope requests the latest release
information from the public GitHub API. This request contains no personal
information beyond what any web request necessarily includes, such as your IP
address, which is handled by GitHub. See GitHub's privacy statement at
https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies.

### 3.3 The two-Mac Cable Tester

The Cable Tester sends test data **directly between your two Macs** over the
cable or local link you are testing. This traffic does not leave your local
connection and does not reach Faini Made or any third party. The data
transmitted is generated test data, not the contents of your files.

## 4. The diagnostic report

PortScope can generate a one-page diagnostic report. **This is a local file.** It
is not uploaded anywhere. It is created only when you ask for it, and you choose
what to do with it.

Because such reports are often pasted into public forums or support tickets,
**redaction is on by default**, removing your machine name, cable identifier, and
display serial number. The export screen shows you exactly what the report
contains before you save or copy it. If you turn redaction off, you are
responsible for what you then share.

## 5. Information we receive

Because PortScope has no accounts and no telemetry, the only information Faini
Made may receive about you is:

- **purchase and license information** from Lemon Squeezy, such as your email
  address, order details, license key, and the names of activated machines,
  which we use to fulfil orders, provide support, and enforce license terms; and
- **anything you choose to send us**, such as the contents of a support email.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it except with the
service providers named in this policy or where required by law.

## 6. Retention

Purchase and license records are retained for as long as needed to support your
license, meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes.
Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle your request.
Data stored on your Mac is retained until you delete it.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
port personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its
processing, or to withdraw consent. Residents of California and certain other
U.S. states have rights under state privacy laws, including the right not to be
discriminated against for exercising them; **we do not sell or share personal
information for cross-context behavioural advertising.** Residents of the
European Economic Area and the United Kingdom have rights under the GDPR, for
which our lawful bases are performance of a contract (licensing and support) and
our legitimate interests (preventing license abuse).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. Note that
information stored only on your Mac is under your direct control and can be
deleted by you at any time.

## 8. Children

PortScope is a professional hardware diagnostic tool and is not directed to
children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under
13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the limited
information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure,
and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. International transfers

Faini Made Inc. is based in the United States. Our service providers may process
information in the United States and elsewhere. Where required, we rely on
appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last
updated" date above and published with the release to which they apply.
